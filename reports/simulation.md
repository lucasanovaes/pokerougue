All rates in this table use every generated run as the denominator. Conditional rates, exact boss reach/clear counts, and termination metrics are in simulation.json.

Bot results are not human win rates.

Rates for each boss use all generated runs as denominator; conditional rates use runs reaching that boss.

Upgraded profile buys all four ranks with 30 endowed research. Collected-shiny adds one perfect-IV shiny starter, not a full retained team.

Collection-3 and collection-6 use endowed mixed collections at IV 18 with no upgrades. Collection-6-shiny uses the same roster with six perfect-IV shinies and no upgrades. These fixtures never grant Pokémon in the actual game.

Enemy levels and guardian team size use starting lineup size only; catches, IV quality and shiny ownership do not dynamically scale opponents. Guardian XP is divided across its team to avoid extra-opponent XP snowballing.

Baseline is the frozen pre-progression game. Same seed names and route stream across cohorts.

Bot limitations: fixed HP thresholds and no tactical switching can produce non-monotonic cohort rates (notably Bellsprout at boss 1); stronger stats are not evidence that this heuristic makes better decisions. Later bosses remain substantially harder and need human playtesting.
