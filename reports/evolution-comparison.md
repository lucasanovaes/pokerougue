Material findings: first-guardian fresh clears remain 74.7–85.6%. Zubat gains the largest solo benefit (2.9% → 18.7% full clears). Ordinary mixed-six lineups increase from 26.3–43.7% to 36.5–68.9%; perfect-shiny six-member teams now clear 92.5–98.2%. Evolution restores expected power progression but widens later starter differences and reduces normal-mode headroom for optimized collections. These are explicit follow-up balance risks, not proof of engagement. Some cohorts can fall slightly because this fixed heuristic changes choices after evolution; it does not tactically switch.

Do not compensate by increasing every enemy level or removing evolution. Next tuning should examine species move budgets, guardian type/counter coverage, and optional difficulty. Validate with human sessions alongside simulation.
